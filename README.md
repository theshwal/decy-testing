# DECY — Alpha testing feedback

Welcome to the public feedback space for **DECY**.

DECY is an early-stage mobile app designed to help people stay ahead of the everyday decisions that matter: household equipment, repairs, contracts, subscriptions, recurring expenses, replacement timing, and practical next steps.

The goal is not to turn life into a task list. DECY tries to make the next useful decision easier to understand:

```text
What matters now?
What can wait?
What should I prepare?
When should I review this again?
```

## What this repository is for

Use this repository to share alpha feedback about:

- bugs or crashes;
- confusing screens or wording;
- translation issues;
- ideas for improvement;
- alpha access requests;
- real-life situations where DECY did or did not help you decide.

This repository is public so testers can report issues without needing access to the private development repository.

## What this repository is not

This repository does **not** contain the source code.

Please do not post:

- private personal information;
- full addresses, phone numbers, contracts, invoices, license plates, banking details, or sensitive documents;
- API keys, tokens, passwords, or screenshots containing secrets;
- confidential business information;
- medical, legal, or financial emergencies.

If you want to describe a real situation, anonymize it first.

## Current alpha focus

The current alpha is mainly about checking whether DECY is understandable and useful in practical situations:

- adding a household item or equipment;
- seeing why a topic comes back as important;
- deciding whether to repair, replace, compare, wait, or prepare;
- understanding contracts and subscriptions;
- using reminders to come back at the right moment;
- identifying where optional AI assistance may help later.

Some AI-related elements may appear in the interface. During alpha, these may be active, limited, experimental, disabled, or marked as future capabilities depending on the test build.

## How to report feedback

Open a new issue and choose the form that best matches your case:

- **Bug report** — something does not work.
- **Alpha feedback** — general feedback after testing.
- **Confusing screen / wording** — something is unclear.
- **Feature idea** — suggestion for later.
- **Alpha access request** — ask to join the alpha test.
- **Language / translation issue** — translation or wording problem.

Helpful feedback usually includes:

- what you were trying to do;
- what happened;
- what you expected;
- your device type;
- your app language;
- whether the issue blocked you or was only confusing.

## Response expectations

This is an alpha project. Replies may not be immediate, and not every suggestion will be implemented.

Issues may be:

- accepted for internal review;
- linked to a private internal task;
- marked as not planned;
- closed if they are duplicates, unclear, or outside the current scope.

## Privacy reminder

This repository is public. Anything posted here can be read by anyone.

Keep your reports practical, anonymized, and focused on the app behavior.
