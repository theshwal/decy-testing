# DECY — Alpha testing feedback

Welcome to the public feedback space for **DECY**.

DECY is an early-stage mobile app that helps people stay ahead of practical decisions that cost money, wear out, renew automatically, or are easy to forget.

It is designed to help answer simple questions:

```text
What matters now?
What can wait?
What should I prepare?
What should I compare, stop, repair, replace, or review later?
```

DECY is not a banking app, not a task manager, and not a marketplace. The goal is to make the next useful decision easier to understand, at the right moment.

---

## Current alpha focus

The current alpha is focused on clarity, usefulness, and real-life decision flow.

We especially want feedback on:

- onboarding: do you understand what DECY is for?
- adding a household item or equipment;
- adding a contract, insurance, or subscription;
- understanding why something appears as important;
- deciding whether to keep, compare, repair, replace, stop, or review later;
- reminders and time-based decisions;
- confusing words, screens, or flows;
- language and translation issues.

Some AI-related features may appear during alpha. They may be limited, experimental, disabled, or available only with an alpha access key and quota. DECY must remain useful without AI.

---

## How alpha access works

The alpha is currently invitation-based.

Depending on the test phase, you may need:

- an alpha access key;
- a short request through the app;
- manual approval before access is enabled.

Please do **not** post private access keys publicly in this repository.

If you want to request access, use the **Alpha access request** issue form and share only the minimum information requested.

---

## How to report feedback

Open a new issue and choose the form that best matches your case:

- **Bug report** — something does not work.
- **Alpha feedback** — general feedback after testing.
- **Confusing screen / wording** — something is unclear.
- **Feature idea** — suggestion for later.
- **Alpha access request** — ask to join the alpha test.
- **Language / translation issue** — translation or wording problem.

Helpful feedback usually includes:

- what you were trying to do;
- what happened;
- what you expected;
- your device type;
- your app language;
- whether the issue blocked you or was only confusing;
- screenshots only if they do not reveal private information.

---

## What not to post publicly

This repository is public. Anything posted here can be read by anyone.

Please do **not** post:

- full names of third parties;
- postal addresses;
- phone numbers;
- personal email addresses other than your own contact email when requesting access;
- contract numbers;
- invoices;
- bank details;
- license plates;
- access keys;
- API keys, tokens, passwords, or screenshots containing secrets;
- sensitive documents;
- confidential business information.

If you want to describe a real situation, anonymize it first.

Good example:

```text
My home insurance contract was shown as something to compare, but after choosing “keep for now” I did not understand why it came back later.
```

Bad example:

```text
Here is my full insurance contract with my address and customer number.
```

## Response expectations

This is an alpha project. Replies may not be immediate, and not every suggestion will be implemented.

Issues may be:

- accepted for internal review;
- linked to a private internal task;
- marked as duplicate;
- marked as not planned;
- closed if they are unclear or outside the current alpha scope.

The private development repository remains private. This public repository is only for tester feedback.

## Project status

DECY is still in alpha. Expect rough edges, incomplete flows, changing wording, and occasional bugs.

The most useful feedback is not “this is not finished”, but:

```text
I tried to do X.
I expected Y.
The app showed Z.
This was clear / confusing / blocking because...
```

Thank you for helping make DECY clearer, calmer, and more useful.
